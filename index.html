Anmatos

A private personal assistant for one person.

Anmatos is a personal companion that helps its owner remember things, keep track of plans and answer questions about his own life. It runs on the owner's own computer and is used only by its owner. There is no public sign-up and no other users.

With the owner's permission it can read his own Gmail in read-only mode to keep his personal memory up to date. It never sends, deletes or changes e-mail.
